Customer Information

Delivery & Shipping Policy

We aim to make delivery clear and convenient. Please review the information below before placing an order.

Delivery Coverage

BlagSheik accepts delivery requests from customers across Bangladesh. Delivery availability will be checked and confirmed when you place your order.

Delivery Charges

Delivery charges depend on the customer's delivery location. We will confirm the applicable delivery charge with you before dispatch. The delivery charge is separate from the product price.

Estimated Delivery Time

Delivery time may vary depending on your location, product availability, and courier service. Please contact us for an estimated delivery timeframe for your address before confirming your order.

Order Confirmation

Submitting an order through WhatsApp or Messenger sends an order request; it does not automatically confirm your order. BlagSheik will contact you to confirm product availability, final product price, delivery charge, and delivery details.

Before Your Order Is Dispatched

Please make sure your name, phone number, and complete delivery address are correct. We will confirm the order details with you before dispatch.

Need Help?

If you have questions about delivery to your area or the applicable charge, contact us before placing your order.
