Customer Care

Return & Exchange Policy

We want you to receive the product you ordered in good condition. Please read our return and exchange guidelines below.

When You Can Request a Return or Exchange

You may contact BlagSheik within 3 days of receiving your order if the product:

  • Arrives damaged;
  • Is defective; or
  • Is different from the product you ordered.

How to Request Help

Please contact us on WhatsApp within 3 days of receiving the item. Include your name, order details, a brief explanation of the issue, and clear photos or a video showing the problem where possible.

Keep the product and its packaging until we respond. Our team will review the information and explain the next steps.

Review and Resolution

Return or exchange requests are reviewed based on the issue reported. We will discuss the available resolution with you before asking you to send anything back. Please wait for our instructions before returning a product.

Questions or a Return Request?

Contact BlagSheik within the 3-day period so we can assist you.
