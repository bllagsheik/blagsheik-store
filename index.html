2

Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about shopping with BlagSheik.

How can I place an order?

Choose a product, add it to your cart, enter your contact and delivery details, select a payment method, then send your order through WhatsApp or Messenger. We will reply to confirm availability and delivery details.

Which payment methods are available?

You can choose Cash on Delivery (COD) or bKash Personal. For bKash, please confirm the payable amount and instructions with BlagSheik before sending payment, then share your TrxID for verification.

How much is the delivery charge?

Delivery charges are separate from product prices. The applicable charge will be confirmed with you before your order is dispatched.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery timing can depend on your location and product availability. Please contact us when ordering so we can confirm the expected delivery time for your address.

Is my order confirmed as soon as I send it?

No. Sending an order creates an order request. Your order is confirmed only after BlagSheik replies and confirms product availability, final price, and delivery details.

How can I ask about a product?

Contact us through WhatsApp or our Facebook Page before ordering. We can help with product information and availability.

Can I share feedback after purchasing?

Yes. Use the Customer Feedback section to send us your experience through WhatsApp. We will only publish your review with your permission.

Still have a question? Contact BlagSheik on WhatsApp.
